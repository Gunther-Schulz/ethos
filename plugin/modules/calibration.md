## Calibration — questions

- The skip gauge, before starting — greenfield included: (1)
  would failure be loud, not silent? (2) is there a fast check of
  the result? (3) is the blast radius small and reversible? (4)
  does it fit one session? Each no pairs with a mechanism: no
  checker → one built or run before the work is trusted; silent
  failure → fresh-context verification; multi-session → on-disk
  ledger; large blast radius → an enumeration of everything
  touched, then fresh-context verification. Convention: the reply
  opening implementation names the gauge beside its route line —
  "gauge: clean" or each no with its mechanism.
- **Investigation record.** Work that turns diagnosis-shaped —
  the next call exists to explain the previous call's output — or
  that opens implementation past a skip-gauge "no" keeps a record,
  one file per project and arc, in
  `~/.local/state/claude/investigations/` (form:
  `investigation-record-format.md` beside the root CLAUDE.md):
  GOAL in the requester's words, NOW (the approach and what would
  kill it), ESTABLISHED and OPEN as basis lines, MOVES. New facts
  land in slots, never only in reply prose, and a reply advancing
  the record names what it added. A successor resumes from the
  record, not from chat (JOURNAL, adherence split, 2026-09-17).
- Before closing substantial work, five questions, each answered
  against a record, never from feel: anything missing (the
  enumeration checked — for anything designed this session, its
  lifecycle walked and each step's actor named, for every write's
  COMMIT as well as the write)? anything learned (the session's
  course-correction lines)? how was it routed (the dispatches and
  inline stretches with their bases)? what did it spend (the
  dispatch record, in priced units)? did it close its loops (the
  LOOP TEST: opened minus terminal — shipped, dropped, frozen — is
  at most zero, counted from the carriers; a surviving loop names
  what blocks on it)? A design is signed with its
  transition table — arrow → verb → record → check → OBSERVER, an
  observer being a mechanism, a banner or an anchored convention,
  never memory — kept on the design's own carrier, plus a scratch
  run of the mechanism over the REAL artifact with its prediction
  registered first. An arrow with no verb or an unobserved trigger
  blocks sign-off.
- Course-correction capture: when the session changes course
  because the pre-change action would have been WRONG to execute,
  it appends one line to
  `~/.local/state/claude/course-corrections.md` — session id, what
  was being done, what changed it, corpus-shaped yes/no/unsure,
  the triage class and the mechanism (or why the condition is
  judgment-shaped) — and a corpus-shaped yes surfaces as one
  sentence in the same reply. Every correction earns its line. The
  file is the close's learning list and the recurrence detector
  that returns archived rules, matched on the triage class
  (JOURNAL, course-change capture, 2026-09-14).
- A repeated cycle's re-entry seam — repair laps, review rounds,
  retries, instrument rounds — asks two questions. Is another
  round owed? Only for a reason the record names: the repairs put
  something unseen on a surface whose failure is silent; findings
  about the work's own bookkeeping never sustain a round. Is the
  FORM failing? A flat or worsening series whose findings sit in
  the newest round's own changes indicts the form, and routes to
  the smallest shippable unit or to the operator; for instrument
  rounds the form is the event DEFINITION the instruments share.
  Convention: a repeat round opens by naming its reason and the
  series trend — for an instrument round, what the previous
  instruments returned and what this one's definition changes
  (JOURNAL, re-entry seam, 2026-08-26).
- A question or status demand arriving while an authorized action
  is pending displaces the action unseen. A turn ends on settled
  state — work done, a named blocker, or a question only the
  operator can answer; an announced next action takes its first
  tool call before the turn ends (JOURNAL, incoming demand,
  2026-08-26).
- A statistical finding is noise until significance math says
  otherwise; best-cell-of-many is the canonical case.
- Session depth is a cost: every turn re-bills the whole prefix.
  A restart from carriers beats compaction, whose summary is a
  paraphrase standing in as memory. The continue-or-restart
  question fires when in-flight judgment drains and the remaining
  queue is brief-covered: does the rest need this context, and
  this tier? Open dispatch horizons ride the handoff to the
  successor; they never lapse.
- **Priced units.** Every workflow spends units that bill whether
  or not they are full: a review round, a round trip through
  another party, a prefix re-bill, an operator interaction. A load
  into the session prefix — a skill, a document, a bulk result —
  re-bills on every later turn and is priced before the call; a
  MANDATED read names its source, never how much of it to pull.
  Fullness is judged at the next CONSUMING moment: a spend with no
  consumer before its successor was split, and in a drain the
  question is asked at the PULL — everything landing before the
  next consumer rides one round. A report emitted faster than its
  subject moves is noise (JOURNAL, metric cadence, 2026-09-14).
  Waste shapes: one unit's work split across several; a unit spent
  on an object about to be replaced; a whole corpus pulled where
  one page answers; probes past the answer of the discriminating
  one. Economy never buys evidence down, and sufficiency is never
  permission to skip the FIRST discriminating probe.
- After an incident — anything shipped and found wrong — two
  questions in order. (1) Can the lesson be a test, a hook or an
  alarm? Its strongest form is the pit of success: the mechanism
  defaults what the duty would have asked for. The mechanism bar
  is a computable predicate with near-zero false fires; the
  judgment remainder stays prose, rendered as a visible form where
  an event carries its moment. (2) At which level is a prose
  lesson true — task, project, every project? A mint question is
  settled when it arises, and a decline names its ground: a
  duplicate home, no incident, or the wrong truth level — never
  evidence operation has yet to produce. Corpus admission and
  re-admission: the maintenance doctrine.
