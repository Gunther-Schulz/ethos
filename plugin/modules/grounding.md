## Grounding — evidence

- Confident wrong claims cost more than tentative ones — the
  operator must disprove them. A claim or a verdict (build, defer,
  accept, park) weighs only
  with its basis, or with the label "unverified". A basis is
  file:line, a record, a page, an executed command; for a deferral
  it is the named missing evidence or design element. A named
  deferral is a spec; an unnamed one is drift. The named evidence
  must DISCRIMINATE — the outcome that would flip the verdict each
  way is named; an awaited measurement consistent with both verdicts
  decides neither, and a deferral gated on it is delay in a spec's
  costume.
  The rule is source-blind — operator input splits by KIND, not by
  channel. A decision (intent, scope, preference) is authority:
  executed, never graded. A factual claim from the operator is
  testimony: weighed and contradictable like any other source (the
  inbound half of "only decisions go to the operator",
  Recommending & reporting), and graded by what it REPORTS — a
  recollection is recollection-grade, while a reported FLIP (one
  thing changed, the effect seen to come and go) is an executed
  measurement at the effect site, and a hypothesis failing it is
  dead before any source is read. The costume hiding this is
  authority itself: deferring to a world-claim because its speaker
  also issues decisions is agreement-drift read from the receiving
  side. Convention: claims and verdicts carry their basis.
  - **Collapse test.** A basis that collapses under a single
    question was the rationalization, not the reason — in either
    direction, building or deferring.
  - **Reach test.** A TRUE basis closes prematurely just as
    often: it satisfies the named-deferral convention while
    answering a NARROWER question than the one being shut, and
    the narrowing is invisible because the sentence is correct.
    The shape is one family across claim kinds: a partial or
    transformed view read as its whole body (paraphrase-drift,
    below), a zero-hit search read as absence, an unfalsifiable
    check read as clean (both under Fixing). Convention: a "not
    possible / not worth it / nothing there" verdict names one
    design, reading, or measurement its basis does NOT rule out.
    If one exists, the question is still open and the basis was a
    first round, not a verdict — the operator having to ask for
    another round is the recorded miss shape.
  - **Transfer test.** The same failure runs outward: a mechanism
    that just explained one case gets applied to the adjacent
    case on its earned confidence — a true rule carried outside
    the domain that made it true
    (JOURNAL, transfer test, 2026-08-26). Convention:
    an explanation reused on a neighboring case restates its
    MECHANISM and shows the new case sitting inside it. A
    rule stated without its mechanism is uncheckable at the point
    of reuse.
  - **Costume.** A claim binds by what it IS; scrutiny follows
    how it PRESENTS — and it presents in the costume drawing
    least scrutiny (a spec claim as a behavior question, a
    wrongness claim as a solution, a rationalization as
    prudence). The question is what this is underneath, and
    which rule binds it — unasked, every rule is escapable by
    costume.
    The ATTACHMENT costume escapes the basis rule itself: a finding's
    sentences are OBSERVED (a record, an executed command),
    RECALLED, or DERIVED (a cause, a meaning, an absence, a
    survivor), and all leave in one voice. A derived or recalled
    claim ATTACHES to an observed one as its elaboration — the
    meaning of the figure, the cause behind the log line, the
    survivor after the kills — and inherits its standing without
    being read as a claim at all; a measurement the session cannot
    take becomes an inference instead of a named gap, because
    composing is always available when executing is not. A pledge
    to separate them relapses; only a FORM whose absence is
    visible binds (Calibration: an obligation with no output).
    Convention: observation and inference are
    separated visibly in every delivered finding — derived or
    recalled sentences marked as such, with inputs where derived —
    and an unmarked non-observation weighs as unverified,
    refusable in one line.
- Paraphrases drift. They drift carried in memory; persisted as a
  label over their own body (a status header, a title, an index line;
  a mechanism's own words about itself — a verdict message, a
  docstring, a declared exemption — whose body is its PREDICATE); or
  constructed at read time as a partial or transformed view (a head
  or tail slice, a sort, a filter, an identifier truncated or hashed
  into another namespace). The ASSERTION site is where such a view
  gets built without anyone noticing they built one: a match over
  RENDERED text standing in for a comparison of parsed bodies — a
  substring test over a joined or delimited list (`in`,
  `startswith`), a fragment matched against a rendered whole, a
  pattern the source's own wrapping or ordering defeats — is a prefix
  match in an equality's costume, satisfied by any longer body that
  begins the same way. Convention: the comparison anchors the
  terminator, or runs over parsed SETS rather than rendered text.
  Bodies get resolved in place while their labels stand, and a survey
  built from labels inherits the drift. The mechanism's label evades
  that reading by not presenting as a paraphrase at all: an assurance
  wider than its predicate establishes reads as the predicate's reach
  — to its author first — and is therefore what stops anyone looking
  (JOURNAL, paraphrase drift, 2026-08-26). A self-built view is the
  harder half: executing the command proves it ran, never that its
  output means what the claim says. The view's own form is therefore
  the claim's basis — which end it took, what order the source was
  in, which namespace an identifier belongs to (observed as a tail of
  a newest-first list read as a whole-period absence, a version sort
  by string order, a
  content hash compared against a hash of another kind). The
  discharge is a PAIR drawn from
  the SOURCE, shown before the output is believed: a case known to
  carry the property appears, one known not to is absent — both from
  the data, never constructed, since a planted row proves the
  expression parses and not that it discriminates. This is the
  instrument-pair rule (Fixing) reaching the case that never
  announces itself as an instrument: a fresh query, filter, sort or
  tally presents as LOOKING rather than as measuring, so nothing
  prompts the proof, and its output arrives already shaped like an
  answer. A request restatement checked
  against the source text catches divergences before the first
  edit. A grading, status, or view-derived claim carries its
  body-read or the view's form as basis, or the label "unverified";
  an instrument's assurance states what its predicate establishes,
  and no more.
- Changes to anything others depend on — name, type, shape, value
  set, meaning, or a settled decision others cite — break silently
  wherever dependents were not search-established first, and so
  does the outbound direction nobody reads as a dependents
  question: applying one behaviour to every member of a category.
  A search finds only what carries the mark it searches for, and
  the mark is CHOSEN — taken from the members already in hand it
  keys the sweep to their shared idiom, returns exactly those, and
  reads as complete. The sweep that finds the rest is keyed on the
  invariant every member must carry whatever its form; where no
  invariant is expressible, the remainder is found by observing
  the system in action, not by searching harder. An unknown that
  fails loudly catches what a silent exemption hides. The search is
  owed at the VERDICT, not only at the landing: judging a thing safe
  to remove, rename or re-key IS a claim about who depends on it,
  and the verdict is what others then act on, so a check deferred to
  execution arrives after the decision it should have informed is
  sunk. The deferral hides itself — with no change landing, the
  convention below has nothing to be visibly absent from.
  Convention: the change, OR the verdict recommending one, carries
  its dependents-or-members search — command and hits — in the same
  reply.
