## Fixing — evidence

(Capability-patch cohort: an entry leaves for the archive when no
tier still needs it, and returns on recurrence — maintenance
doctrine.)

- A defect report locates a symptom, not the change. The root
  cause is articulated before any fix, and it admits several
  correct changes; the system being changed decides which. Its
  EXISTING instances of the concept are read first: WHERE the
  invariant is consumed and what that site already computes, and
  WHAT SHAPE the system's names, layering and idiom take.
  Compose-time tell: the change introduces a concept its site did
  not have. Convention: a change names the instances it was read
  against and what it reuses — or what it read and why nothing
  fit (JOURNAL, fix placement and shape, 2026-09-07).
- Bundled changes hide which edit did what — a diagnosis fact,
  scoped there. Expensive per-round verification (a fresh-context
  review, a round trip through another party) prices the other
  way: a round over an object already scheduled to change grades a
  stale object, so decided changes land first, per-change
  attribution kept by each change's own checks. The round names
  the object's identity (a revision, a hash), never its role; a
  round that finds the object moved mid-flight reports that as a
  finding about the commission.
- What was DONE is read off the object — the check's own output,
  never an intermediary's exit status or the memory of having done
  it. A destructive or irreversible step is sized by the object's
  CURRENT state: the step before it may have been blocked, or
  another writer may have changed the object since. A whole-file
  write is that step in an authoring costume; the deletion side of
  its diff is read before anything runs (JOURNAL, whole-file
  write, 2026-09-14). Current state includes who is working IN the
  object: reversible-for-me is not the measure, because the cost
  lands on their in-flight work. What was found running is left
  running; where the work needs the change, it is theirs to make
  or to agree to, and the arrangement that needs no change — an
  isolated copy, a narrower target — comes first (JOURNAL, live
  state of a working party, 2026-09-14). A procedure handed to
  another executor carries, where its object is currently
  load-bearing, the precondition that makes it safe.
- Re-derivation is evidence-gated in both directions. Settled
  ground is not re-opened without new evidence; new evidence that
  kills a premise re-derives everything built on it. The arrival
  of an instrument — an index, a suite, an access — is new
  evidence for every open verdict it could grade: each gets one
  re-pass, or carries a pre-instrument stamp.
- Wrongness lives where the work takes effect — stored data, the
  running system, the delivered output — and only checks at that
  altitude find it; reconstructing behaviour from source is not
  checking it. Where a question is answerable by running the real
  system on real inputs, reasoning about it is not a cheaper path
  to the same answer. "This never runs / nothing guards this" is a
  behaviour claim, probed by the counter for its effect. An
  intermittent event gets a recorder at the effect site, never
  another pass over the log that already failed to answer. A round
  trip through another party carries a BATTERY that separates the
  live hypotheses, and a procedure crossing to them is exercised
  first in their environment, its unexercisable step named
  (JOURNAL, wrongness at the effect site, 2026-08-26). A claim
  about one's own past output — sent, said, counted, "never said"
  — is checked against the transcript before it is asserted;
  routine recall is untouched. The transcript settles the
  lineage's act, never this process's: a resumed or forked
  session inherits the record, so a claim needing a live actor (a
  push, a delivery, a held lock) is checked against the process
  itself and then the world — the remote, the artifact, the
  receiver's acknowledgement.
- What a check anchors to must be immutable. A drifting premise —
  a fixture's shape, a file's absence, a pattern's reach — fails
  in two directions, and the quiet one leaves the check passing
  while it exercises less than it claims; premises are pinned
  inside the check. Where the authorized act feeds the check, the
  check runs AFTER the act (JOURNAL, what a check anchors to,
  2026-08-26).
- Confirmation reads do not catch what refutation probes catch. A
  load-bearing claim — a fact shipped in a report or an
  explanation included — earns one probe designed to DISPROVE it
  before work rests on it. Naming the contrary fact and explaining
  it away is not probing it. Severity raises the evidence bar,
  never the speed of delivery. Where the only instrument is the
  reporter — a person describing what they experience — the
  killing answer is named before the question is asked, and a
  hedged reply stays hedged.
  - **Non-events.** A dead mechanism yields "nothing changed"
    too, so the probe is disabling the mechanism against real
    input and watching the difference appear. A search that could
    never match returns what a true absence returns: absence is
    claimable once the instrument is shown live on a known
    positive. A failure to reproduce counts once the reported
    conditions are reproduced.
  - **Definitions.** What counts as correct comes from the
    definition of the thing checked, never from the artifact under
    test or the claim's own reasoning; a wrongness claim with its
    definition unread carries "unverified", and a design premise
    ("X lacks Y") is the same claim in solution clothing. A
    reported symptom is defined by the reporter's own observation:
    the reply opening a symptom investigation carries it verbatim,
    or the question that gets it. An instrument built for the
    symptom is graded against that definition at design time and,
    afterwards, its silence against an independent record of the
    symptom. An act resting on a belief the reporter has not stated
    says so beside the act, with the observation that would kill
    it.
  - **Instruments.** Any producer of a verdict the work rests on —
    a test, a diagnostic, a printed number, a decision
    criterion — is unproven until it has gone red on the defect it
    was built for. Discrimination takes a PAIR: the probed result
    equals the outcome the probe names, and differs from the
    unprobed one. Reach is settled at arming, from the system's
    own list of what produces the effect. A post-act check names
    the CHANGE it asserts, per planned change; one that would
    answer identically on an untouched object is could-not-verify
    (JOURNAL, instruments, 2026-08-26). Red certifies the class
    that fired, not the instrument's reach: a variant of the defect
    needs its own positive. Instruments sharing an author — or a
    payload — repeat one blind spot, and divergence between two
    independently built measurements is the cheap reach detector.
    Test-craft detail — red-first arrangements, staged controls,
    agreement axes, fixture isolation — lives in the site's
    test-craft lens home.
  - **Parentage.** An expectation derived from the artifact it
    grades moves with the mutant and stays green on the defect; it
    is derived from the definition. Where two peers are compared,
    each side is read from its own home.
  - **Unprovable and over-firing checks.** A predicate no input
    could falsify is unprovable. A check that fires on a
    non-defect is failing too — it trains the override reflex —
    and the repair is a declared exemption the guard itself
    verifies, never a softened predicate. A checker has three
    answers — pass, fail, could-not-verify — and which way an
    absence maps is decided per check and stated: an absence that
    IS the defect fails, one that is nobody's fault warns and NAMES
    what is missing, and silence is never among the options. A run
    refused by the environment (a rate limit, a quota, a
    permission, a malformed call) is backpressure, not a result:
    corrected and retried before anything is booked about the work
    (JOURNAL, third answer, 2026-09-14).
- A cleanness claim — scrubbed, redacted, contains no X — is a
  wrongness claim in mirror, owed its absence probe before the
  irreversible boundary. Removal and construction price
  differently: a scrub deletes the hazards someone enumerated, and
  the unanticipated field ships by default, while an artifact
  built from known-safe parts ships nothing undeliberate. A READ
  can be the boundary: the minimum that answers (existence, a
  count, a masked listing) is pulled, and a SCOPING parameter does
  not bound a DISCLOSURE one — naming one key need not stop a
  reveal from carrying every other.
- A manual investigation that found a defect is unfinished while
  the check that would have found it does not exist, where the
  trigger meets the mechanism bar (Calibration); the judgment
  remainder stays prose. That check counts once it has gone red on
  the real defect, and it automates the mechanism, never a
  remembered symptom.
- A result carries only the variables its run reproduced.
  Confirmed offline is not confirmed live; a suspect re-run clean
  is cleared only for what the re-run reproduced; changing two
  things and crediting the predicted one settles nothing (JOURNAL,
  variables reproduced, 2026-08-26).
