# Corpus maintenance discipline

Read before editing the operational corpus (membership criterion:
Edit discipline, Corpus-wide amendment audit) or settling a
mint/no-mint verdict on it: a "don't mint" that settles the question
is a maintenance act too, and delivered without this doc loaded it
skips the triage machinery on the gate-free path (no edit, no hook).
This file is maintenance doctrine — never loaded during normal
sessions. Its operational traces: the root CLAUDE.md's pointer, a
closing pointer in each corpus skill, and a pre-edit gate that
demands a same-turn read of this file before a corpus edit; the
modules carry none.

This is the PORTABLE core — the doctrine that holds wherever the
corpus is used. A site running the corpus may keep a SITE OVERLAY
beside its own carriers, which fills in this file's role-names with
that site's real paths, carriers and instruments (its journal
carrier, its item carrier, its governed set, its enforcement
mechanisms). The overlay extends; it never restates. Where this
file names a ROLE, the overlay names the artifact filling it; a
rule here that needed the overlay to be executable would be a
self-containment failure (Composition rule) and belongs in neither.

## Self-governance (locked shape)

This file binds itself to the corpus rules it applies — density,
Pareto, amendment-over-addition, neighbor + touched-entry scope,
provenance, self-containment, consolidation (the governor is not
exempt) — with TWO deliberate deviations, both register facts
(skill-craft, the two registers): the render test and the
no-directives form do not apply here, because this is GATE text for
one maintainer at edit time, and gate text takes the directive
register. Shape lock, binding on THIS file (the portable core; a
site overlay is a flat clause-keyed list and carries no section
shape of its own): a Self-governance preamble plus four sections —
Stack, Composition rule, Edit discipline, Review cadence — and new
content lands inside the four; needing a fifth section IS a
structural rewrite and takes that process (Edit discipline).

## The stack (who governs whom)

Four layers, one responsibility each:

1. **External model-behavior evidence** — verbatim snapshots of
   vendor guidance on how steering text lands. A site keeps its own
   snapshots and names them in its overlay; the layer exists
   whether or not any given site has one.
2. **skill-craft** (plugin) — the authoring craft for steering
   text; absorbs (1) as its evidence base.
3. **This file** — governance of the corpus; binds (2) as the
   vetting standard for every mint.
4. **The operational corpus** (membership: Edit discipline,
   Corpus-wide amendment audit) — the layer sessions load (the root
   and its enabled modules always, the skills on demand: dispatch
   hook-demanded at dispatch, executor named first in briefs to
   cheaper tiers); product of (1)–(3).

Each layer is amended at its own home; an authoring lesson minted
at the wrong layer is the truth-level failure (Calibration),
applied to layers instead of projects.

## Composition rule (what belongs in the corpus)

Philosophy (operator decision): the corpus informs judgment, it never
decides — the agent decides, under a standing delegation included,
and the operator overrides at veto-gates. The trust split beneath the
verification rules: a model's JUDGMENT is trusted to decide; WHETHER
a check is owed stays priced by stakes and the skip gauge — most
reversible work owes none; and where one IS owed, it is never the
producing context's own self-assessment — a fresh context or a
mechanism runs it, and this does not relax as models improve
(self-preference is measured capability-independent). The operator
holds a role, never a remainder (the kind split — reporting module):
intent, preference, authority over the irreversible — exercised as
decisions at seams and as deliberation with the desk, whose settled
conclusions always take a carrier (two exits).

- Content forms: **evidence** (facts + observed failure shapes;
  environment-bound facts are bindings, staleness-stamped),
  **conventions** (protocols for consistency and cheap oversight),
  **questions** (calibration prompts). No directives, no
  ask-instructions.
- **Render test:** if an entry can be obeyed, it is mis-rendered —
  re-render as the evidence that justified it, a convention, or a
  question; if it must HOLD regardless of judgment, it precipitates
  into a hook (veto-gate, mechanism bar permitting — Edit
  discipline, Precipitation first) and leaves this file. A
  convention is rendered as a FORM whose omission is visible in the
  output (a line, a label, a slot), never as a resolve: the
  weakest rendering measured is the session's own in-reply pledge,
  which did not survive its next paragraph with the rule it
  restated loaded (skill-craft, the anchored trigger — source
  label).
- **Pointer exemption:** navigational pointers and staleness stamps
  ("read X first", "re-check when Y changes") are structure, not
  directives — they route loading and mark expiry, they decide no
  work.
- **Gate criterion:** a veto-gate belongs where wrong-decision cost
  is high AND harm outruns operator reaction. Gates receive made
  decisions (veto voice, never ask voice).
- **Durability classes:** evidence entries are capability patches
  (expected to shrink as models improve; retired on lack of firing
  evidence at fire-rate reviews) or bindings (staleness-checked,
  never fire-checked; a condition that is an executable check
  carries no state snapshot beside it). Conventions persist while
  their consumer exists. Enforcement lives in hooks, pruned only on
  architecture change. Since the modular split the classes are
  module-grain too: each module's class sits in the root's table;
  what a class means for ABLATION is review machinery (Review
  cadence, Ablation). Placement: a binding lands beside the rule it
  conditions, in the layer owning that rule (dispatch-harness facts
  in the dispatch skill, machine facts in the corpus's environment
  sections) — never pooled into a collected bindings home, which
  divorces mechanism from rule and drifts.
- **Consumer calibration:** prescription density follows the
  consuming model of the entry. The corpus is loaded by every
  session and subagent tier: entries a stronger model no longer
  needs are stamped as capability patches (durability class) rather
  than deleted while weaker tiers still read them; forms destined
  for briefs to cheaper tiers stay prescriptive. Full rule:
  skill-craft (stack layer 2).
- **Failure triage before any mint:** classify the failure — gap
  (nothing covers it) / unloaded (existed, wasn't read) /
  loaded-but-inert (read, didn't fire). Only a gap justifies new
  content; unloaded → fix loading; inert → derive the mechanism of
  the non-firing BEFORE any verdict (what did the rule's trigger
  read as, that the incident escaped it?) — the sharpen lands on
  that mechanism, and "inert but nothing improvable" with no
  mechanism named is a basis that collapses under one question
  (Grounding) — then sharpen the evidence there or precipitate to
  a hook.
- **Density test:** every sentence must change behavior — if
  deleting it changes nothing, it goes.
- **Scope precedes default:** a qualifier lands at or before the
  statement it scopes, never as a downstream carve-out.
- **Self-containment:** a rule's MECHANISM lives in the rule — a
  reader with none of the operator's plugins installed can execute
  it. External docs may be cited as provenance or further reading,
  never as the missing half of a procedure ("borrow X from tool Y"
  is a dead rule the day Y is renamed, moved, or absent). Legitimate
  cross-references: the stack layers this doc itself declares
  (skill-craft as vetting standard), routing DESTINATIONS (a repo's
  own lens home — the rule works even if the destination changes),
  and bindings, which are environment-bound by
  definition and staleness-stamped. A SITE OVERLAY is not an
  exception to this rule: it fills role-names with local artifacts,
  and a portable rule that cannot be executed without reading it
  has failed this test here, not in the overlay.
- **Lens count near zero — the ethic/lens test at mint time.** The
  corpus holds ethics (stances informing every task, firing
  continuously and invisibly — no firing statistics needed) and
  stamped bindings; shape-specific mitigations ("when doing X, watch
  for Y") are lenses, however valuable — they tax working memory on
  every unrelated task and belong in project CLAUDE.mds, a repo's
  own lens home, or hooks. Mint test: ethic → corpus
  candidate; environment-bound fact → binding, stamped; situational
  check → route out. Abstraction probe, run BEFORE routing out:
  restate outside coding with no term swapped for a domain word — a
  failed restatement routes to a lens home, a successful one stays
  (routing first loses entries that would have survived). Fire-rate
  reviews judge capability patches, not ethics.
- **Distillation boundary (protocol machinery vs corpus).** From a
  multi-pass protocol, what distills into the corpus is the
  one-pass judgment mechanic (refute-before-build,
  execution-shaped enumeration, amendment cascade); the guaranteed
  LOOP never distills — prose executes once in a mood-carrying agent,
  a loop that must hold is machinery, and the skip gauge
  (Calibration) routes between the two. Boundary test: would one
  conscientious application have caught the motivating incident?
  Yes → corpus; only iteration caught it → machinery (canonical
  shape: a falsification round killed a decision the prior round had
  confirmed deep into its candidate list — no one-pass rule
  reproduces that).

## Edit discipline

- **Generalize upward before appending.** When an incident reveals a
  rule under-fired, first widen or restate the existing rule at the
  principle level — enumerated triggers always have gaps. Append an
  instance only when the general form demonstrably under-fires.
  Upward includes ACROSS DOMAINS: every mint or revision runs the
  abstraction probe (composition rule, ethic/lens test) as a standing
  drafting step, not only at borderline disputes — draft the
  domain-free form first, keep domain vocabulary only where it is a
  binding or where the general form loses precision the work needs.
  Standing goal (operator decision): the corpus is robust for any
  kind of work, domain-specific only where necessary and high value —
  domain vocabulary is usually habit, not information. The probe as a
  word-swap passes a draft that narrowed in SUBJECT: a rule written
  from the incident's most vivid instance, pulled toward the
  claim-type its enforcement form can check (a finding about
  inference drafted as a rule about numbers, because numbers can be
  tagged) — every domain word swapped, the rule still firing on one
  instance of a class it was meant to name. The reach check on the
  draft is the same as Grounding's on a verdict: enumerate the
  incident's SIBLING instances (the other sentences of the same
  output that share the failure) and the governed set's recorded
  shapes of the class (grep the observed parentheticals), and test
  whether the drafted text fires on each — an instance it misses
  names the rule's true subject, and the draft widens before it
  lands. The mechanical half of that enumeration is already in the
  corpus: every INBOUND "(Module, handle)" pointer into the entry
  names a case the entry must fire on, so the pointers are read with
  the siblings (measured: one module's three entries each failed that
  read, the trigger not firing on the case a sibling routed to it by
  name; a dangling inbound handle is the same defect one step
  earlier, and a phrase search over a hard-wrapped corpus is blind to
  wrap AND case until both are normalised). The journal line carries
  the result as a slot (instances tested / fired / missed), so a mint
  that skipped the check is visible in its own record rather than at
  the next incident.
- **Amendment over addition:** revise in place > merge > extend >
  add new. Also an EVIDENCE rule, not only a preference order
  (operator decision): a sharpen inherits the base rule's earned
  standing and grounding — the seat is already paid for — while a NEW
  entry needs its own named grounding observation and does not land
  without one; ONE observation suffices for either. NO mint waits on
  firing counts: the fire-rate review is retirement machinery, never
  an admission gate (build-first, below), truth-LEVEL is decided by
  the abstraction probe, not by cross-project firing statistics — the
  posture is mint-and-tune-in-operation, never wait-for-recurrence.
  The tell that a mint is really a sharpen: the failure triages as
  loaded-but-inert, not as a gap (composition rule, failure triage).
- **Neighbor-collision check:** every mint or amendment — vetted or
  build-first — reads its home section's neighbors and tests for
  contradiction on a concrete case, and puts the touched entry's OWN
  full text in scope for the render, density and provenance tests —
  an edit audits its host, not only its delta; clean it or park the
  cleanup with a pointer, never leave it silently (operator
  decision). Neighbors are not only spatial: before a mint lands,
  SEARCH the governed set (membership: Corpus-wide amendment audit,
  below) for existing statements of the rule being added — grep the
  concept, not just the wording (the Grounding dependents-search rule
  applied to the corpus; its search-KEY caveat — the mark is CHOSEN —
  binds here too); a hit means amend or source-label, never a silent
  second home (the semantic sibling outside the read radius is how a
  duplicate home is born). The search runs BOTH directions: beyond
  restatements of the rule being added, a mint that changes an
  operating premise re-scopes every rule RESTING on the old premise —
  ask which existing entries would forbid, mis-fire, or fall silent
  under the new arrangement, and land those sharpens in the same edit
  set; a delta-only review finds duplicates, never the rules the
  delta invalidates. The same pass grades COHERENCE, both ways: the
  edit joins the fabric it enters — the corpus's existing terms (one
  term per referent), an existing mechanism reused where one carries
  the rule, the level of its nearest kin — and the fabric it touches
  is re-graded against the CURRENT corpus: a neighbor gone subtly
  stale under later optimization, or superseded by a better-shaped
  rule, is harmonized or queued for disposition in the same edit set
  (clean or park with a pointer, the exit above); disharmony is
  individually defensible at each edit and only ever caught wholesale
  at consolidation, too late. Evidence cannot command, only
  contradict: a contradiction is resolved in the text (which fact is
  true, at what scope) before the mint lands.
- **Triple probe on the fresh mint** — after the rule text stands,
  three passes in sequence, each grading the text the previous
  pass corrected: CARRIER (does the home outlive the occasion,
  and does its future reader exist? a rule in a dying carrier is
  no mint), SHAPE (does each sentence claim exactly what is
  true — no overclaim, load-bearing clauses unpacked from shared
  breaths, the parent rule cross-referenced instead of restated),
  ABSTRACTION (do the wording AND the subject survive the
  incident — occasion counts, quoted excuses, and act-narration
  cut where the shape-sentences already carry the failure class;
  the reach check over sibling instances run, Generalize
  upward). The mechanism
  is attention-concentration: a single-axis pass finds what a
  mixed-attention read leaves, and each repair changes the text
  the next pass grades (observed: three sequential passes on one
  fresh rule surfaced three distinct real defects where the
  preceding mixed reads had surfaced none; no combined-review arm
  was run — the sequencing claim is inference from that record).
- **skill-craft vetting:** every mint or amendment additionally runs
  skill-craft's authoring checks against the FINAL RENDER, not the
  rule idea — the render's own noun phrases are where leaks live:
  each sources from corpus vocabulary, domain literature, or session
  context, and session context is the leak signal.
- **Corpus-wide amendment audit:** the operational corpus is the
  thin root CLAUDE.md plus its modules, and the dispatch and
  executor skills (SKILL.md and references/). A site names the
  exact paths in its overlay; the CRITERION is here and the paths
  are there, because the criterion travels and the paths do not.
  The two skills are the sending and receiving sides of one
  discipline and share its brief-facing forms, so a rule amended in
  either is audited across both. A sibling `worktree` skill is NOT
  corpus: portable git mechanics, governed by skill-craft alone. A
  rule deliberately restated in more than one home carries a source
  label at each restatement; amending it means auditing every home —
  including the EXECUTABLE ones that carry corpus rule text (hook
  docstrings, the form detectors), canonical for their own lanes
  though outside the `.md` set a path matcher covers. This
  paragraph is the membership CRITERION; a site that enforces
  membership mechanically keeps ONE executable shadow of it, which
  every keyed mechanism derives from — so changing membership is one
  edit there, never a hunt for the mechanisms that encode it.
- **Pareto:** every edit shows fewer words or more coverage; name
  what was removed or consolidated — as a `Pareto:` line in the
  edit's commit body, the form whose absence is checkable: named
  nowhere visible, the answer under-fires exactly at busy mints
  (measured: a same-day burst landed a 16-line amendment whose
  honest line would have read "nothing removed", caught only by
  the operator's density challenge at review). Neither → the edit
  goes back for its merge or its cut.
- **Precipitation first.** Before writing prose, ask: can this be a
  CI test, hook, or runtime alarm instead — and can it live at
  project level instead of globally? The mechanism bar is
  Calibration's (source label): computable predicate, near-zero
  false fires — precipitate the computable slice, leave the
  judgment remainder prose. A global hook taxes every matching call
  in every session (the lens argument applied to enforcement): a
  narrow situational guard lands at project or plugin scope.
  The same question prices PLACEMENT: the always-loaded set (root
  + enabled modules) is one document at context-load price
  (skill-craft, the two loads) — content servable from an
  on-demand home (the dispatch skill for rules consumed after its
  gate-forced load, a project file, a lens home) does not land in
  the always-loaded set.
- **Verdict jurisdiction:** a maintenance verdict names the ACT it
  rules on — mint, sharpen, consolidate, retire, no-mint — and cites
  the rule governing THAT act; one basis stretched over two different
  acts reads as discipline while being drift (the costume principle's
  maintenance rendering — Grounding). Semi-check at verdict
  time: the cited rule's own text naming a different act than the one
  being decided means the jurisdiction is wrong. The shape: a
  consolidation-timing rule, which governs compression, stretched
  over an extraction, which build-first governs — one operator
  question apart.
- **Build-first (operator decision):** a well-formed rule candidate
  is minted DIRECTLY — operator GO, or the GO of a judgment desk
  holding a standing operator delegation whose scope covers the
  corpus work, that delegation's line QUOTED in the same journal line
  so the authority is checkable where the mint is recorded — and such
  a delegation counts only where the operator stated it first-hand in
  the RECEIVING session, since a delegation carried by the desk that
  exercises it is testimony about a decision and never the decision,
  which is the one shape this rule cannot afford — one commit, plus a
  journal line marking it candidate-in-operation. Tuning and
  retirement happen in operation via the fire-rate review, not by
  pre-approval. The journal line rides in the SAME commit as the
  corpus edit. Where a site can enforce that pairing mechanically it
  does, and the pairing is only mechanizable where carrier and corpus
  share one index: a corpus file and a journal carrier in DIFFERENT
  repositories cannot be checked as a staged pair, and a gate
  spanning them can demand no more than that the carrier be staged in
  its own repo at commit time — an assurance no wider than its
  predicate (Grounding). Where no mechanism reaches, the journal line
  is convention, written in the same session as the edit. The line
  also names durability class where not timeless — a clause bound to
  current tool behavior carries its retirement trigger — and the
  reach slot (Generalize upward).
- **Growth is review-judged, never mint-blocked (operator
  decision):** additions land when they are justified; the check on
  cumulative load is the review regime, not a commit gate. Every
  fire-rate review RECORDS the measured always-loaded total (word
  count over the root plus enabled imports) in its journal entry
  beside the tripwire reading, so growth is a measured trend with a
  per-review keep-or-displace verdict over the delta — not a felt
  one. The total is recorded in its OPERATING rendering too, because
  a word count understates what the corpus costs by two multipliers
  nobody carries in their head: it is re-read on EVERY call of EVERY
  lane, so its bill is the total times calls times lanes, not the
  total once. The review measures its own terms rather than carrying
  a ratio — an instrument reading real transcripts reports them, and
  the same run gives the crossover above which splitting a read-only
  fan-out is cheaper than one long lane. A site carrying such an
  instrument names it in its overlay with the terms it measures; a
  formula whose symbols are bound nowhere the reader can reach is
  decoration, and decoration in a gate file is the density test's own
  failure case.
- **Process for structural rewrites** (composition-rule changes,
  large restructures, corpus-wide re-renders, and any SPLIT or
  MOVE of this file's own governed text): amend-first on
  operator GO — land the draft same-session, then run the
  fresh-context vet against skill-craft as a CHECK on the landed
  draft (the brief MUST state: task-universal scope,
  self-containment, this composition rule; for re-renders
  additionally the clause-survival diff per skill-craft's
  rendering-from-a-source rule), fix findings before push,
  dispositions recorded in the commit or journal. A pre-approval
  round-trip only
  when the operator asks for one (operator decision: amend early,
  test in production — same posture as build-first). Exception
  (operator decision): skill-craft's canonical files review at their
  own SEAM CADENCE — one fresh-context self-review before the release
  that delivers a change-set, machine-read semantics mandatory at
  that seam, notable/nit findings to the operator (skill-craft,
  Reviewing a skill). The vetting standard the stack leans on is
  unchanged; what moved is WHEN it fires — per release, no longer per
  commit. This file and the corpus stay per-edit: live-on-write
  carriers deliver at the edit itself.
- **Provenance:** corpus rendering of the general rule "rationale
  goes where future sessions look" (Insurance) — here,
  sessions load the files for operation and the maintainer reads
  the journal and commits at fire-rate reviews; in a repo whose docs
  ARE what future sessions read (e.g. a personal manual), the same
  general rule places incidents in the docs — re-derive placement
  from it, never port this rendering. Within this corpus:
  patch-class entries cite their motivating incident in the commit
  message (or the journal), not in the file — this covers ALL
  incident narration in the governed set AND this file. In-file, an
  incident survives only DE-PARTICULARIZED: its shape, stripped of
  dates, occasion-identifying counts, and named artifacts (a dated
  occurrence inside a rule goes stale as prose while staying true as
  history). Measurements are not particulars — they are evidence or
  bindings and carry stamps. In-file dates are legitimate only as
  bindings' staleness stamps ("as of", "valid while") — this gate
  file excepted, which keeps its own attributions. Elsewhere a fiat
  rule reads "(operator decision)" at most (operator decision); its
  date and authorship live in the journal or commit.

## Review cadence

- **Fire-rate review** every ~2 weeks, or after an incident burst:
  which rules fired (cite the occasions), which never fired
  (retirement candidates — capability patches only; ethics and
  bindings are not fire-judged), which are SUPERSEDED — every
  entry stands at disposition, no class exempt, because a corpus
  that only adds is the default failure: fire-silence retires
  patches, but a convention whose consumer no longer exists, a
  rule a later mechanism or better formulation has absorbed, and
  an ethic re-rendered more truly elsewhere retire on THAT
  evidence at any review (supersession-judged is not
  fire-judged — the ethics/bindings exemption above shields them
  from firing statistics only, never from disposition) —, which
  bindings are stale — and a
  sampled grading of dispatch decisions against the dispatch-log
  (route chosen vs outcome); a session never grades its own
  routing, so that sample routes to a fresh context (Insurance,
  fresh-context verification). The review also records the
  interval's META-FRACTION — apparatus-serving volume (corpus,
  hooks, dispatch machinery and their repos) against object-level
  work, by a stated instrument, never by feel — beside the
  always-loaded total: the apparatus is graded
  against the work it serves, not only against its own doctrine.
  Meta-dominated two reviews running flips the review's
  default verdict from consolidate to cull, and new governance
  machinery in that state takes an explicit operator GO.
  Evidence source: the corpus's JOURNAL carrier — read it before
  proposing amendments; it declares this consumer, so the chain must
  stay unbroken. A site names that carrier in its overlay, and
  names it in ONE place: a journal cited here by role and by path
  in two homes is two bodies for one fact.
  Read the corpus-parked items in the same pass: they live in the
  site's item carrier under the corpus goal — the goal filter is
  the selector, and this review is the consumer that read serves.
  An item leaves as a mint (the journal records it) or is dropped
  with a one-line reason. Project-specific items carry other goals
  or live in their own project's carrier, never under the corpus
  goal.
  The same pass asks which PROJECT-file rules have GENERALIZED
  since the last review: the accretion module already mints a
  generalizing correction upward at the correction moment; this
  question is that rule's cadence backstop, because a project
  CLAUDE.md accumulates globally-true rules that no correction
  moment ever re-visits.
- **Ablation — what a durability class means, and the grain at
  which it can be measured at all.** The review's instrument is
  the WHOLE-CORPUS arm: a CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR copy with every import
  removed, against an unmodified side, at least two repeats per side,
  markers pre-registered over OUTPUT and TRAJECTORY — never
  self-report, which both sides produce alike. Finer grains are not
  weaker but below resolution (measured over 18 arms): five per-entry
  pairs discriminated nothing, a whole module removed (16 entries,
  3,621 words) discriminated nothing, and all eight imports removed
  DID, markers stable across repeats on both sides. A
  cross-referencing corpus has small margins at every sub-corpus
  grain, so a per-entry or per-module null grades the INSTRUMENT,
  never the entry, and licenses no retirement; those arms are the
  standing baseline. The MODULE toggle survives only as a
  live-interval experiment, and eligibility is GRADED per entry for
  it — a module toggles only when every member's grade permits it: an
  entry guarding a silent failure (attribution trailers, booking and
  carrier rules) blocks its whole module — quiet weeks cannot grade
  it — while an entry whose absence is loud (route line, gauge line,
  closing harvest) permits a tuning interval. Module classes
  (composition rule, durability classes) are the defaults, and a
  mixed module takes its strictest member's rule: ethics dominate and
  bindings are never ablation subjects, so a module carrying either
  is not toggled whole, and one mixing loud conventions with silent
  guards is toggled only after the silent guards move out or with the
  experiment explicitly accounting for them. The toggle:
  backtick-wrap the module's import in the root and record the
  interval in the journal; re-enable is the reverse edit. That a code
  span is not import-parsed is UNVERIFIED — the discriminating probe
  (a backticked import in a scratch project, loaded-or-not against an
  unwrapped positive control) is owed before the next TOGGLE
  interval; the whole-corpus instrument above does not inherit that
  debt, removing imports rather than wrapping them.
- **Consolidation pass — trigger: growth, not calendar.** Scope: the
  whole governed set (Edit discipline, Corpus-wide amendment audit)
  AND this file — the governor is not exempt, and a day of
  individually-justified edits can double a doc. When a doc has
  grown materially since its last consolidation (rough tripwire:
  +30% lines or a burst of same-day mints), the next fire-rate
  review runs a compression pass
  over it: merge rules sharing a mechanism (one probe referenced
  twice beats two statements), re-render accreted narrative to the
  evidence register, re-run the density and Pareto tests on every
  entry touched since. Measure then cut — a burst of fresh mints
  consolidates after an operating interval, not the same week it
  landed. The measured always-loaded total (Edit discipline,
  Growth is review-judged) is read at every pass: the trend is
  the waterline instrument, the pass does the structural half —
  merging, re-rendering — that a word count cannot. The pass carries
  the tripwire's BASELINE: it records its own post-pass total in its
  journal line, and the next trigger computes against that figure,
  never against git archaeology.
